
                                  How to Lock Recent Apps in Xiaomi on MIUI 12


  If your smartphone is Xiaomi Redmi/Poco on MIUI 12 or newer, to lock apps, do it like this:

Step 1: Open the Security app.

Step 2: Tap the settings icon in the top right corner.

Step 3: Select the Speed Booster.

Step 4: Then scroll down and tap App Lock.

Step 5: Enable any apps that you want to lock.

Step 6: Done, then the app that is locked in the recent app will be marked with a padlock icon on the top right.
